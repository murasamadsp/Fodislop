Terrain decal atlas

The runtime asset is Assets/Textures/terrain-decals.png. It is exactly 128×32 RGBA: four horizontal slots of 32×32.

SlotPixelsContent
0x 0–31Branching crack
1x 32–63Pebbles
2x 64–95Stone fragments
3x 96–127Dust and grit
  • Keep the background fully transparent. Alpha controls decal blending.
  • Keep every mark inside its own 32×32 slot; the shader samples texel centres.
  • Use hard pixel edges and no mipmaps. Rotation and mirroring are applied by the shader.
  • The slot order and atlas dimensions are runtime contracts. Artwork inside a slot may be replaced freely.
